# QA-C: UX, UI and accessibility (2026-10-13)

_Read-only audit by a haiku-worker (brief Q1). Saved and spot-verified by the architect: P1-3 (no caller of `cancel`), P1-8 (no `BackHandler` anywhere) and P1-9 (`textFaint` values) are confirmed. Contrast ratios come from `src/ui/tokens.ts`. Web screenshots show no horizontal overflow at 400 px. Player, library and settings on real data, TalkBack, 200 % font and hardware back are untested on a device; findings that depend on them are marked hypothesis._

## P1
1. **Estimate shows "≈ $0.00" when prices are unknown** (offline, or the model isn't in the catalogue): `core/pipeline/estimate.ts:189-210`, `adapters/catalog.ts:14-19`, `features/shared/format.ts:34-41`. `add-chapter-screen.tsx:166-172` has no `.catch`. Fix: an `unknown` estimate state, "Can't price this offline", explicit confirm. M.
2. **Re-generate audio is a one-tap paid action** with no confirm or price (`series-screen.tsx:127-130`). Fix: ConfirmSheet with the estimate. S.
3. **No way to stop a running chapter**: `cancel()` exists in the facade, with no UI caller (confirmed). Fix: Stop in the Processing section and the chapter menu, with confirm copy that says what is kept. S-M.
4. **A failed row says "Try again" with no button** for non-retryable `UNKNOWN` (`chapter-row.tsx:117` vs `chapter-view.tsx:137-143`). Fix: one policy and matching copy. S.
5. **Long-press-only actions are invisible to TalkBack**:
   - chapter menu: `chapter-row.tsx:73`
   - series menu: `series-card.tsx:45`
   - sentence actions: `synced-text-pane.tsx:318-343`
   - previous sentence: `transport.tsx:36,45`

   Fix: a visible ⋯ (48 dp) plus `accessibilityActions`, and sentence spans as buttons. M.
6. **Mini player nests a button in a button** (`mini-player.tsx:76-113`, hypothesis). Fix: siblings. S.
7. **Vocab empty state describes a gesture that doesn't exist** (`vocab-view.tsx:45`). The real flow is to hold a sentence, then Save to Vocab. S.
8. **Sheets ignore Android back** (no `BackHandler`, confirmed). Fix: in `Sheet`, back calls `onClose`. S.
9. **`textFaint` is below 4.5:1 in both themes**, yet carries meaningful text. Fix: dark `#8E8980`, light `#6F695F`, keep the old value for decorative use. S.
10. **Light-theme fields and controls are nearly invisible** (1.04 and 1.24:1). Fix: a `boundary` token (light `#8F877A`, dark `#6B7182`) for control outlines. S.
11. **Language picker `FlatList` inside a bottom sheet** may not scroll (hypothesis). Use `BottomSheetFlatList`. S.

## P2
1. Light success/warning/active-chip text below 4.5. Use success `#2A7A4B`, warning `#8A6500`, accent on soft `#875010`. S.
2. Touch targets are 44, and the bar is 48 dp (`tokens.ts:111` + literals). S-M.
3. Font scale is capped at 1.35. WCAG wants 200 %. Fixed heights must become minimum heights first. M-L.
4. Sentence sheet at 46 % with no scroll; actions fall off on small screens or large text (hypothesis). S.
5. `Card` drops `accessibilityLabel`; the vocab delete action sits on a non-focusable wrapper. S.
6. Ambience cue row label is not applied (missing `accessible`). S.
7. Text pane: duplicate "Open text" labels; the sentence is announced as "Open text". S.
8. Sheets have no modal semantics and no focus move. S.
9. Scrubber accessibility increments use a stale position (two quick steps give one). S.
10. Android keyboard relies on `adjustResize` under edge-to-edge (hypothesis). S-M.
11. Onboarding ignores reduce motion. S.
12. Onboarding has no Back, and the legal notice comes after the key step. S-M.
13. An action toast (Connect OpenRouter) auto-dismisses after 3.5 s. S.
14. A draft chapter is a dead end ("Not processed yet", no action). S.
15. Create is disabled with no reason given. S.
16. No "Waiting for connection" state (overlaps N3). M.
17. Text-pane auto-follow `scrollToIndex` without `getItemLayout`, and failures are swallowed (hypothesis). M.
